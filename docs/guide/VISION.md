# Vision: a North Star for post-linguistic communication

## The Adriatic Moment

You stand on a cliff above the Adriatic at evening. The sea has gone gold, pine resin and salt
are in the air, and you feel something very specific: serenity and melancholy at once, with a
shape and a temporal contour. You text a friend: *"The sunset was beautiful."* Six words. They
will never feel what you felt.

Words are pointers into a codebook the receiver already carries, such as language, culture,
memory and embodiment. When that prior is shared, language is extraordinarily efficient. When it
is not, as across cultures, generations, disciplines, or between people and machines, language
is a famine.

## The wager

ESP makes part of that codebook **explicit, trainable, interoperable and consent-governed**. A
moment of experience travels as six typed parts:

| Part | Carries |
|---|---|
| KNO | knowledge |
| INT | intention |
| EMO | emotion |
| CTX | context |
| SEN | sensory qualities |
| TEM | timing |

Each part is a vector from a shared, auditable encoder, plus human-readable anchors and relations.
Language does not disappear. It becomes **one renderer of meaning** next to visualization, machine
action, immersive media and, one day, neural interfaces.

## Why typed parts change everything

- **Consent becomes precise.** Share what and why, and keep how it felt. Consent is signed,
  time-limited, revocable, and it is enforced by the wire itself: withheld parts are absent.
- **Privacy becomes measurable.** Leakage between parts can be audited, attacked and improved.
- **Machines become accountable.** Agents already exchange hidden states. ESP gives that traffic
  identities, capabilities and a causal audit trail *before* human experience becomes payload.

## The horizon, stage by stage

| Stage | What becomes possible | In this repository |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** content, teams, AI | semantic media retrieval, director's-cut tracks, intent-carrying collaboration, typed context for language models | built and tested |
| **L2** body and senses | physiology-aware VR, rehabilitation, crew synchronization | adapters, features, calibration, 30-min soak |
| **Machines and agents** | handover with intention and timing, governed agent-to-agent latents | MEB and ESP-Agent profiles |
| **Shared Semantic Memory** | encrypted experience capsules, recall under fresh consent, legacy policies | XCF v1, gate, tombstones, trust vector |
| **Typed Hive** | collective cognition without merger: pooled knowledge, context and intention; emotion never averaged | episodes, secure aggregation, FROST with DKG, emergence audit |
| **L3 neural** | device makers decode neural signals into typed parts | decoder interface boundary, neural adapter contract |
| **L∞** | persistent, interoperable semantic memory; exchange through substrates that need not be linguistic | the invariants are ready |

The realization of L∞ is speculative, but the design target is deliberate. If those substrates
mature, type identity, consent, provenance, revocation, compatibility and auditable privacy
should not need to be reinvented. That is the point of building them now.
