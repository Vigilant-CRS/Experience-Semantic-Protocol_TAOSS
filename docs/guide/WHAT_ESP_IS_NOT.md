# Scope: what ESP delivers today, and where the frontier is

ESP is a **protocol and a reference implementation**. It moves typed, consented representations
of experience and makes their privacy measurable. The following boundaries keep that promise
precise, and each one is also where the next contribution fits.

| Area | What ESP delivers | Where the frontier is |
|---|---|---|
| Experience | Structured representations from self-reports, content, sensors and encoders | Phenomenal experience itself is not transmitted; the Adriatic Moment is an operating-regime argument, not a claim of telepathy |
| Emotion | Content-side affect, self-declared affect, and subject-side inference only at L2 with consent (never in EU workplaces or schools, Art. 5(1)(f)) | Validated affect models on real, consented data (claims level 4) |
| Independence of types | Leakage *measured* with MI, probes and V-information; covert channels hardened | Encoders that reduce cross-type leakage further; the reference encoder still leaks masked emotion (R² ≈ 0.78) |
| Privacy | Masking, typed DP, metadata protection, covert-channel audits | Colluding receivers and model inversion stay under active measurement |
| Evidence | Claims level 3: wire, semantics and adapters verified | Preregistered H1–H3 studies on real corpora (levels 4–5) |
| Neural interfaces | Decoder interface boundary and adapter contract | Device and open-dataset adapters (level 6 with partners) |
| Language | Always available as a renderer | Measuring how much meaning typed transport adds on top of language |
| Law | Enforces the *declared* deployment (EU AI Act guard, DPIA hooks) | Counsel and the project's legal review decide the rest |
| Quantum resistance | No post-quantum claim: v1 is declared classical-only | Hybrid channels once the transport stack can prove them |

See the [claims ladder](../CLAIMS.md) for how every public statement is tied to evidence.
