# What ESP is not

- **Not mind reading.** ESP moves *representations* that a sender, an encoder or a measurement
  produced. It cannot see thoughts.
- **Not consciousness transfer.** V13 says so explicitly: ESP does not claim to transmit
  phenomenal experience.
- **Not an emotion detector.** Physiology yields *features* (heart rate, skin conductance), not
  emotions. Subject-side affect is an *inference*. It is labelled `inferred_subject`, needs
  profile L2 and explicit consent, and is refused for biometric emotion recognition at work or
  school (EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f)).
- **Not a replacement for language.** Language remains a first-class renderer, and every frame
  has a text rendering. The risk of linguistic atrophy is documented as not technically
  mitigable.
- **Not proof of independence between types.** TAOSS types are *approximately* orthogonal.
  Leakage is measured (mutual information, probes, V-information), never assumed to be zero.
- **Not a privacy guarantee by itself.** Masking removes objects from the wire, and DP adds
  calibrated noise. Metadata, colluding receivers and model inversion are addressed by separate,
  measured mechanisms (metadata protection, T19 audits), not by the type system alone.
- **Not evidence yet.** H1–H3 are falsifiable hypotheses. Smoke benchmarks are exploratory. Only
  preregistered runs with all baselines count, and human studies need ethics approval.
- **Not legal advice.** The regulatory layer enforces a *declared* deployment. Counsel and the
  project's legal review decide the rest.
